 $$ P\left\{X_{1}=0,X_{2}=1\right\}=P\left\{X_{2}=1\right\}=\frac{10}{100}=0.1, $$ 

 $$ P\left\{X_{1}=1,X_{2}=0\right\}=P\left\{X_{1}=1\right\}=\frac{80}{100}=0.8, $$ 

 $$ P\left\{X_{1}=1,X_{2}=1\right\}=0. $$
