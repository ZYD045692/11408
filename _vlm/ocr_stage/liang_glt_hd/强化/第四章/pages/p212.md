(3) $ Z=X^{2}+Y^{2} $的密度函数.

【解析】(1) $ \mathrm{cov}(X,Y)=E(XY)-E(X)E(Y) $，又

 $$ E(XY)=\iint_{D_{xy}}xy\cdot\frac{2}{\pi}(x^{2}+y^{2})\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=0, $$ 

其中  $ D_{xy}=\left\{(x,y)|x^{2}+y^{2}\leq1\right\} $，又

 $$ E(X)=\iint_{D_{xy}}x\cdot\frac{2}{\pi}(x^{2}+y^{2})\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=0, $$ 

 $$ E(Y)=\iint\limits_{D_{xy}}y\cdot\frac{2}{\pi}(x^{2}+y^{2})\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=0, $$ 

故  $ \mathrm{cov}(X,Y)=0 $.

(2) 当 -1 < x < 1 时，

 $$ \begin{aligned}f_{x}\left(x\right)&=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}y=\int_{-\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}^{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}\frac{2}{\pi}(x^{2}+y^{2})\mathrm{d}y\\&=\frac{2}{\pi}x^{2}\cdot2\sqrt{1-x^{2}}+\frac{2}{\pi}\cdot\frac{y^{3}}{3}\bigg|_{-\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}^{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}\\&=\frac{4}{\pi}x^{2}\sqrt{1-x^{2}}+\frac{4}{3\pi}\left(1-x^{2}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}},\end{aligned} $$ 

故  $ f_{X}(x)=\left\{\begin{aligned}&\frac{4}{\pi}x^{2}\sqrt{1-x^{2}}+\frac{4}{3\pi}\left(1-x^{2}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}},&-1<x<1,\\ &0,& 其他.\end{aligned}\right. $

同理, 得  $ f_{Y}(y)=\left\{\begin{aligned}&\frac{4}{\pi}y^{2}\sqrt{1-y^{2}}+\frac{4}{3\pi}\left(1-y^{2}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}},&-1<y<1,\\ &0,& 其他.\end{aligned}\right. $

因为 $ f(x,y)\neq f_{X}(x)f_{Y}(y) $，故X与Y不独立.

(3)  $ Z = X^{2} + Y^{2} $ 的分布函数为  $ F_{Z}(z) = P\{Z \leq z\} = P\{X^{2} + Y^{2} \leq z\} = \iint\limits_{x^{2} + y^{2} \leq z} f(x, y) \, dx \, dy $，则

当z<0时, $ F_{z}(z)=0 $;

当 $ z\geq1 $时， $ F_{z}(z)=1 $;

当  $ 0 \leq z < 1 $ 时,  $ F_{z}(z) = \int_{0}^{2\pi} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{0}^{\sqrt{z}} \frac{2}{\pi} \cdot r^{2} \cdot r \mathrm{d}r = 2\pi \cdot \frac{2}{\pi} \cdot \frac{r^{4}}{4} \bigg|_{0}^{\sqrt{z}} = z^{2} $.

综上, $ f_{z}(z)=F_{z}^{\prime}(z)=\begin{cases}2z,&0<z<1,\\0,& 其他.\end{cases} $
