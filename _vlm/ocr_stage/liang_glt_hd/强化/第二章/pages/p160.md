(2)  $ P\{X \leq Y\} = P\{X < 2\} = \int_{0} \frac{-x^{2}}{9} dx = \frac{1}{27} $.

【例 2.28】在区间 (0,2) 上随机取一点, 将该区间分成两段, 较短一段的长度记为 X, 较长一段的长度记为 Y, 令  $ Z=\frac{Y}{X} $,

(1) 求 X 的概率密度；

(2) 求 Z 的概率密度.

【解析】设(0,2)区间上随机取一数记为 W, 则由题意  $ W \sim U(0,2) $，且

 $$ X=\min(W,2-W),Y=2-X. $$ 

（1）由分布函数的定义有

 $$ \begin{aligned}F_{X}(x)&=P\left\{X\leq x\right\}=P\left\{\min(W,2-W)\leq x\right\}\\&=P\left\{\min(W,2-W)\leq x,0<W<1\right\}+P\left\{\min(W,2-W)\leq x,1\leq W<2\right\}\\&=P\left\{W\leq x,0<W<1\right\}+P\left\{2-W\leq x,1\leq W<2\right\},\end{aligned} $$ 

则

当x<0时, $ F_{X}(x)=0 $;

 $$ F_{X}(x)=P\left\{0<W\leq x\right\}+P\left\{2-x\leq W<2\right\}=\frac{x}{2}+\frac{x}{2}=x; $$ 

当 $ x\geqslant1 $时， $ F_{X}(x)=1 $.

故  $ f_{X}(x)=F_{X}^{\prime}(x)=\left\{\begin{aligned}&1,&0<x<1,\\ &0,& 其他.\end{aligned}\right. $

(2)由分布函数的定义有

 $$ \begin{aligned}F_{z}(z)&=P\left\{Z\leq z\right\}=P\left\{\frac{Y}{X}\leq z\right\}=P\left\{\frac{2-X}{X}\leq z\right\}\\&=P\left\{\frac{2}{X}-1\leq z\right\}=P\left\{\frac{2}{X}\leq z+1\right\},\end{aligned} $$ 

则

当z<1时, $ F_{z}(z)=0 $;

当  $ z \geq 1 $ 时， $ F_{z}(z) = P\left\{\frac{2}{x} \leq z + 1\right\} = P\left\{X \geq \frac{2}{z+1}\right\} = 1 - P\left\{X < \frac{2}{z+1}\right\} = 1 - \frac{2}{z+1} $.

故  $ f_{z}(z)=F_{z}^{\prime}(z)=\left\{\begin{aligned}&\frac{2}{(z+1)^{2}},&z>1,\\ &0,& 其他.\end{aligned}\right. $
